An open-finance concept exploring a neutral view of multiple banks in one app. The idea centres on bringing account balances and transaction information together through consent-based connections, starting with the UAE. NOXX is currently in the ideation phase.

A platform for high-resolution 3D scans and immersive walkthroughs of physical spaces. Terra is designed to help people explore properties and destinations remotely, with applications across real estate, rentals, tourism and hospitality.
